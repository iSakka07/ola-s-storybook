# Ola's Storybook

Build a polished mobile-first interactive romantic story website for a wife named Ola (nicknames: Luluu, Anu). The website is a surprise for her birthday and first wedding anniversary on 04/09/2026. The couple are Abdelrahman and Ola. The experience should take about 5 minutes and feel elegant, modern, playful, intimate, cinematic, and personal — NOT a generic hearts-and-pink Valentine's template. Use tasteful warm neutral colors, deep charcoal, cream/off-white, muted burgundy/dusty rose accents, subtle grain, smooth motion, premium typography, soft shadows, and occasional polaroid-style memory cards. Mobile-first is essential; desktop should also look good.

Core concept: this is an interactive story about their relationship, with the theme of Ola having waited for Abdelrahman through many stages of life, ending with a reversal: 'this time I'm the one waiting for you' and a dinner invitation.

Interaction pattern: mix quizzes, memory rush photo animations, counters, timeline milestones, video placeholders, playful responses, and emotional storytelling. No runaway buttons on mobile. Wrong quiz answers should trigger a witty response and then reveal the correct answer. Correct answers should also get a playful response. Use progress subtly without making it feel like a form.

Audio: first screen should explicitly offer 'Enter with sound' and 'Keep it quiet'. Do NOT bundle copyrighted music files. Create clear placeholders/hooks for legal Spotify or YouTube embeds and for user-owned personal videos. The page must remain fully usable without audio.

STORY FLOW:

1) OPENING
Full-screen elegant dark intro with date 04 / 09 / 2026, then 'Hey, Luluu.' Then Arabic copy: 'النهارده مش Birthday بس… ومش Anniversary بس برضه.' followed by playful line about testing her memory. CTA: START / يلا يا Anusha. Include sound-choice controls.

2) CHAPTER 01 — 2017 / Instagram beginning
Date approximately 07.05.2017. Create a playful Instagram-inspired animation: one like, then more likes, then a fake notification 'Olaa sent you a message.' Arabic copy jokes that Abdelrahman innocently liked several photos and she messaged him. Punchline: 'وبكده بدأت المصيبة.' Show a live day counter from 07/05/2017 to current date with a label like 'Days of Jeje & Zobrobtato'.

3) QUIZ — FIRST DATE
Question in Arabic: 'طيب يا Luluu… أول Date كان فين؟'
Options: 'Liverpool Station' and 'Liverpool Café'. Correct = Liverpool Station. Wrong response: 'Liverpool إيه وخلاص؟ 😂 قريبة… بس لأ يا أنو.' Correct response: 'Liverpool Station ✓'. Then reveal a polaroid placeholder for their first date photo in winter, Ola wearing a red coat. Add caption: 'Red coat. Two kids with absolutely no idea what's coming.'

4) CHAPTER 02 — MILITARY ACADEMY
Date 30.11.2018. Do the first 'Memory Rush': many image placeholders flying rapidly like memories, then abruptly STOP on a featured photo placeholder labelled 'أول يوم كلية'. Show a phone-call style animation: Calling Luluu... 05:00 dropping to ~02:13 with weak signal. Arabic copy: sometimes the whole call was only two minutes because of bad network and limited time, but they were the best two minutes of the day. Mention she waited for his rare leaves and saved her best outfits and plans for when he came back. Add playful sequence 'Abdelrahman v1.0 / v2.3 / Academy Update Installed / Unknown personality detected' to joke that every leave he came back with a new personality. Joke about people telling her 'استنيه بس يخلص كلية وهيسيبك' and have a fake SKIP button / response that leads to 'Spoiler: قرار سيئ يا علا… الراجل لسه موجود لحد 2026.'

Add a live counter for time since 30/11/2018.

5) QUIZ — FIRST GIFT
Question: 'أول هدية من Anusha كانت إيه؟' Options should include 'سلسلة' as correct and a believable wrong choice. Correct response playful: 'ذاكرة محترمة جدًا… لسه في أمل. ✓'. Wrong response: 'سلسلة يا أنو! أنا اللي المفروض أكون بنسى الحاجات دي مش إنتِ. 😂'

6) QUIZ — FIRST HUG
Question: 'وأول حضن؟' Correct = Rowing Club. Add one believable wrong option. After reveal show 'Rowing Club. ✓' then quiet beat and line: 'أكيد فاكرة قلتلك إيه ساعتها…' Do NOT reveal what was said.

7) CHAPTER 03 — OFFICIAL
Memory rush #2 then stop on 10.08.2022: 'قرأنا الفاتحة.' Copy: after more than 5 years, first official step. Show 26.09.2022 as a smaller milestone only, not over-celebrated. Then 12.10.2023 graduation milestone with image/video placeholder. Copy: 'بدأنا وأنا عندي 19 سنة… وشوفتي الرحلة دي كلها تقريبًا من أولها. ووصلنا للنهاية سوا.'

8) CHAPTER 04 — BUILDING A HOME
Create a fast, visual timeline:
20.10.2024 — Cairo, buying her furnishings.
15.12.2024 — first time seeing the furniture.
07.01.2025 — first time seeing their apartment before finishing. Add large apartment photo placeholder. Copy: 'مكنش لسه بيت. كان شوية حيطان وأرض فاضية… بس إحنا كنا شايفينه بيتنا من ساعتها.'
Use a legal audio embed placeholder labelled for the song 'جنة تكفينا - محمود العسيلي' but do not include the music file.

9) MEMORY RUSH #3 — HARD TIMES
Switch tone briefly. Stop on a placeholder for Abdelrahman's back injury. Copy in Arabic: 'كان فيه أيام الموضوع مكانش سهل. كلية… مشاكل… خطوبة… تجهيز… شغل… وحتى لما أنا نفسي وقعت ومقدرتش أقف…' then on next beat: 'إنتِ وقفتي.' Then: 'ودي من الحاجات اللي Anusha عمره ما هينساها.' Keep it restrained, not melodramatic.

10) 22.08.2025 — FIRST WEDDING SUIT FITTING
Bring joy back. Photo placeholder. Copy: 'أول مرة قست بدلة الفرح.' and 'At this point… مفيش رجوع يا Luluu. 😂'

11) CHAPTER 05 — THE DAY / 04.09.2025
Build an emotional crescendo. Rapid dates/images then STOP on 04.09.2025. Show sequence of lines: 'سنين وإحنا بنقول: إمتى؟' then 'إمتى هخلص كلية؟' 'إمتى نتخطب؟' 'إمتى نجهز؟' 'إمتى المشاكل تخلص؟' 'إمتى يبقى لينا مكان واحد؟' Then dark screen: '04.09.2025' followed by 'وأخيرًا… مبقاش فيه إمتى.' then 'بقينا في بيت واحد.'
Add a prominent user-owned wedding slow-dance video placeholder. Label it as the clip where he lifts/spins her during their slow dance. Include a legal external music-embed hook for 'حبيبتي ملاك - عمرو دياب' but do not bundle copyrighted music. After video: 'والأغرب إن ده كان عيد ميلادك…' then 'بس تقريبًا أنا اللي أخدت الهدية.'

12) FINAL QUIZ
Question: 'بعد كل ده، لو رجع بيكي الزمن لـ2017…'
Options: 'هدخل أكلمه تاني ❤️' and 'هعمله Block من أول Like'. If Block: 'قرار منطقي جدًا بصراحة. بس unfortunately الـtimeline اترسم خلاص. 😂' If first option: 'Wrong answer. أنا اللي هدخل أكلمك المرة دي. ❤️'

13) EMOTIONAL LETTER
No more quiz after this point. Use a beautiful slow-reveal reading layout. Arabic copy should convey this authentically and conversationally, not poetic/fusha-heavy:
'Ola, I may not say this often and our relationship was never traditionally romantic. We have always been best friends first. I love you very much. I know my work takes time that should be yours, and I know you often wait for the two days I come back, postpone things, and do everything you can so I return happy. I may not say enough that I see this, but I do, and I appreciate it. From waiting for a two-minute call while I was in the academy to waiting for me to come home from work, you have always waited for me. You stood by me while I was starting, through my hardest periods, and even when I fell and hurt my back. Sometimes I am hard on you and work steals me away more than I want, but I am doing everything I can to make you happy and build a better life for us. I don't love you only because you stood by me; I love you because you genuinely deserve to be loved. You are my best friend, my family, my wife, and the person I have in this world. In my eyes you are the most beautiful and bravest girl. Happy Birthday, اللول بتاعي. Happy First Anniversary, Luluu. وكل سنة وإحنا Jeje & Zobrobtato.'
Make it emotionally strong but natural, broken into screen-sized paragraphs for mobile, with subtle pauses.

14) FINAL TWIST / DINNER INVITATION
After the letter, show: 'بس فيه مشكلة صغيرة…' Then montage of waiting: academy / engagement / home / work. Copy: 'من 2018 وإنتِ تقريبًا بتستنيني.' Show 'مكالمات… إجازات… تخرج… جواز… وحتى دلوقتي بتستنيني أرجع من الشغل.' Then clear screen and large line: 'النهارده بقى دوري أنا أستناكي.'
Reveal a premium invitation card:
DINNER FOR TWO
Luluu × Anusha
04.09.2026 · 06:00 PM
Caviar Lounge
'No quizzes. No waiting. Just us.'
Dress Code: 'تعالي حلوة… يعني تعالي زي ما إنتِ.'
CTA: 'I'm coming 🍣'
On tap: 'ماكنش عندك اختيار أصلًا يا لول. 😂❤️'
Then subtle confetti and final lockup:
'04.09.2025 — We made it.'
'04.09.2026 — Still us.'

MEDIA PLACEHOLDERS / CMS-LIKE CONFIG:
Structure the project so all personalized media can be replaced easily from a single data/config file or obvious assets mapping. Clearly label placeholders for:
- first date red coat photo
- 30/11/2018 academy first-day photo
- academy leave/memory photos
- back injury photo
- 10/8/2022 family/fatiha photos
- 12/10/2023 graduation photo/video
- 20/10/2024 Cairo furnishing photos
- 15/12/2024 furniture photos
- 07/01/2025 empty apartment photo
- 22/08/2025 suit fitting photo
- many random photos for memory rushes
- user-owned wedding slow-dance video

TECH / UX:
- Mobile-first, especially iPhone Safari.
- Smooth but performant animations; prefer transform/opacity, reduce motion when user requests reduced motion.
- Avoid autoplaying media before user interaction.
- Lazy-load media.
- Handle video fallback poster.
- Correct RTL support for Arabic while preserving English labels naturally.
- Counters calculate accurately from actual dates at runtime.
- Keep everything on a single immersive route unless a technical reason requires otherwise.
- No login, no backend needed unless required for simple media config.
- Make all content easy to edit later.
- Use tasteful icons sparingly.
- Build the first complete working version now with placeholders.
